Double Perovskites

Currently, my research focuses on double perovskites, where I am exploring bandgap engineering through controlled doping to enhance their suitability for Solar cells.

Sn (Tin) based perovskites (internship project - IIT BHU)

I have research experience in the Green Energy Physics & Solid-State Ionics Laboratory at IIT-BHU, under the supervision of Prof. Prabhakar Singh. During this work, I synthesized Sn-based perovskite materials using the solvothermal method and carried out their characterization through techniques such as XRD, UV-Visible spectroscopy, impedance spectroscopy, conductivity measurements, and I–V characterization.

DFT Simulations on Nb2C MXene (NITK Surathkal)

Performed density functional theory (DFT) calculations on Nb2C MXene using Quantum ESPRESSO under the supervision of Dr. Kartick Tarafder. Computed electronic band structure, total density of states (DOS), and projected density of states (PDOS) to investigate its electronic properties and assess its potential for optoelectronic and energy-related applications.
